## Featured Story

- **[‘Ahead of his time’: AI helps unlock discoveries hidden in notebooks of man who transformed maths](https://www.theguardian.com/science/2026/nov/13/kurt-godel-notebooks-ai-helps-unlock-hidden-discoveries-man-who-transformed-maths)** <i>Alex Bellos</i>
