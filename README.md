<sub>Featured Story</sub>

- **[Lovable: Many Apps Built on the Platform Expose Sensitive Data](https://www.crissyfield.de/research/lovable-en/)**  
  Researchers examined 159,041 projects built with AI app platform Lovable and uncovered widespread exposure of sensitive information through improperly secured Supabase databases. Among 24,664 websites examined more closely, 7,403 exposed records containing email addresses. Accessible data included identity documents, private messages, contracts, and personal information. The findings highlight the security risks of building applications without sufficient expertise in access controls.

<sub>News & Events</sub>

- **[Nvidia deliberately kept GPU prices high through circular financing deals, report claims](https://www.techspot.com/news/114558-nvidia-deliberately-kept-gpu-prices-high-through-circular.html)** <i>Daniel Sims</i>  
  A report alleges Nvidia financed major AI customers to stimulate demand, absorb excess chip production, and sustain high GPU prices. The strategy reportedly involved deals with cloud providers and AI companies, raising questions about circular financing in the AI industry.

- **[Policy and Disclosure: 2026 Edition](https://projectzero.google/2026/11/disclosure-policy.html)** <i>Natalie Silvanovich</i>  
  Google Project Zero introduces a stricter vulnerability disclosure policy in response to AI-accelerated vulnerability discovery and exploitation. Disclosure deadlines will shrink from 90 days to 60 in 2027 and 30 in 2028, with most grace periods eliminated.

<sub>Articles & Analysis</sub>

- **[Tech Companies Are Trying to Shift the Entire Concept of Privacy (Again)](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/11/tech-companies-are-trying-shift-entire-concept-privacy-again)** <i>Thorin Klosowski</i>  
  The EFF examines how AI-powered smart glasses, wearables, and always-listening devices are redefining privacy. Their ability to record and transcribe everyday interactions raises questions about consent and the rights of people who never agreed to be monitored.

- **[A Family Tree of Software Job Titles](https://zwischenzugs.com/2026/11/03/a-family-tree-of-software-job-titles/)** <i>Imiell</i>  
  An interactive family tree traces 104 software industry job titles from the 1940s to today's AI engineers. It explores how roles such as sysadmin, DevOps, SRE, and platform engineer emerged, evolved, and sometimes reinvented the same responsibilities under new names.

<sub>Guides & Tutorials</sub>

- **[FreeBSD bhyve Live Migration: Moving VMs Between Hosts](https://gyptazy.com/blog/freebsd-bhyve-live-migration-support-rfc/)**  
  A technical walkthrough of an experimental live migration implementation for FreeBSD 15.1's bhyve hypervisor. It covers pre-copy memory transfer, dirty-page tracking, failure recovery, and testing, with observed VM downtime of approximately 138–171 milliseconds under favorable conditions.

<sub>Stories & Opinions</sub>

- **[What happened to 3Com?](https://dfarq.homeip.net/what-happened-to-3com/)** <i>Dave Farquhar</i>  
  How did 3Com, once a dominant networking equipment manufacturer and major Cisco competitor, disappear? A retrospective examines its retreat from enterprise networking, costly diversification into modems, declining profitability, and eventual acquisition by Hewlett-Packard.

- **[Ground Stop Watch: the FAA nationwide ground stop, live and replayable](https://grounds.top/)**  
  An interactive visualization reconstructs the November 13 nationwide FAA ground stop using flight-tracking data. Replay the disruption, compare departures with the previous week, and explore airport recovery patterns and estimated missed departures.
